Prayer Lock vs Pray Screen Time: which Christian app blocker is right for you?

A Pray Screen Time alternative · By Daniil Yarmolenko · Updated November 2026

Pray Screen Time shows a prayer prompt every time you open a blocked app; Prayer Lock protects one dedicated daily prayer time. Both block distracting apps on iPhone through Apple Screen Time, but they answer a different question: do you want a prayer pause every time you open an app, or one protected prayer time each day? Here's an honest comparison.

The core difference

Pray Screen Time is a Christian screen-time app from Manifest Automation. You pick distracting apps, and each time you open one, a prayer screen appears first: a short pause to pray or reflect on Scripture, then the app opens. Its app-lock features are part of a paid tier. As of November 2026 it is one of the most-reviewed apps in this category, with roughly 9,700 App Store ratings and a 4.9 average.

Prayer Lock protects one dedicated prayer time a day. You schedule it, Focus Mode blocks the apps you chose (social media, news, games), and they stay blocked until you finish and tap “I Prayed”. The rest of the day your phone works normally. A daily streak, prayer history and mood tracking show the habit forming over weeks. Blocking runs on-device through Apple's Screen Time API; Prayer Lock never reads the content of your prayers.

When Pray Screen Time is the better choice

If you want a tiny prayer pause every time you reach for Instagram or TikTok, dozens of times a day, Pray Screen Time is built for exactly that, and it has years of reviews behind it. Choose it if the friction-on-every-open model appeals to you and you don't mind that prayer becomes a few seconds at a time.

When Prayer Lock is the better choice

If what you want is a real, unhurried daily prayer time, ten minutes with God rather than ten seconds before a feed, Prayer Lock fits better. You schedule your prayer time, Focus Mode locks the apps you chose, and they stay locked until you tap “I Prayed”. The rest of the day your phone works normally. Choose Prayer Lock when the goal is one protected quiet time, not a toll booth on every app.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pray Screen Time block apps for a whole prayer session?
No. Pray Screen Time gates each app open with a brief prayer prompt and then lets you in. Blocking apps for a dedicated, scheduled prayer session is what Prayer Lock does.

Which is cheaper, Prayer Lock or Pray Screen Time?
Both are free to download with paid features. Prayer Lock costs $39.99/year (with a 7-day free trial) or $7.99/month in the US. Pray Screen Time's app-lock features are in its paid tier; check its App Store listing for current pricing.

Can I use Prayer Lock and Pray Screen Time together?
Technically yes, but both use Apple Screen Time to shield apps, so overlapping rules can be confusing. Most people pick one model: per-open prompts (Pray Screen Time) or a protected daily session (Prayer Lock).
