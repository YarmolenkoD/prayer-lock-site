Prayer Lock vs 'Prayer Lock: Christian Focus': how to tell the two apps apart

Two different apps · Prayer Lock

There are two separate apps called Prayer Lock, made by different developers, and it's easy to mix them up. This page is about Prayer Lock - Quiet Time, the free iPhone app on this site. The other is Prayer Lock: Christian Focus by Mauricio Baron / Covenant Studios, LLC. Both help you pray — here's an honest, neutral rundown of the differences so you can choose the one that fits you.

Why there are two apps with the same name

App names aren't exclusive, so it's possible for two independent teams to land on the same idea and a similar name. That's what happened here: Prayer Lock - Quiet Time and Prayer Lock: Christian Focus are made by different developers, live under different App Store listings, and are simply two separate products that happen to share a name. Neither is a copy of the other; they're both sincere takes on the same good idea — pray before you scroll.

The concept overlaps a lot: both apps let you lock your distracting apps and ask you to pray before you unlock them. So if you're deciding between them, the useful questions are about identity, platform, and pricing, not about one being better than the other.

When the other app might suit you

Prayer Lock: Christian Focus is a good fit if you want a guided experience rather than open-ended prayer time. It's on both iOS and Android today, so if you're on Android it's available right now. Its distinctive feature is personalized, Bible-rooted prayers matched to your mood: you answer a short survey, and it delivers a prayer piece by piece along with a correlating Bible verse. It also tracks a prayer streak and prayer-journey analytics. If cross-platform availability and guided, verse-paired prayers appeal to you — and a subscription fits your budget — it's worth a look.

When Prayer Lock (Quiet Time) is the better choice

Prayer Lock - Quiet Time is the better fit if you want something free, simple, and focused. It does one thing well: it blocks the distracting apps that pull you away until you've prayed, and it keeps a daily prayer streak and prayer history so the habit sticks. There's no subscription and no scripted prayer to follow — it protects a quiet space and leaves the prayer itself up to you. If you're on iPhone and want a free, no-friction way to pray before you scroll, this is it. Android is coming soon, and you can join the waitlist.

Frequently asked questions

Are these the same app?
No. There are two separate apps that share the name Prayer Lock, made by different developers. This page is about Prayer Lock - Quiet Time (iPhone, free). The other, Prayer Lock: Christian Focus, is a different app by Mauricio Baron / Covenant Studios, LLC.

How do I know which Prayer Lock I have?
Check the App Store ID or developer. Our app is Prayer Lock - Quiet Time, App Store ID 6758918601, by the Prayer Lock team. The other is Prayer Lock: Christian Focus, App Store ID 6744703926, by Mauricio Baron / Covenant Studios, LLC, and is also on Android.

What's the difference?
Both block your distracting apps until you pray. Prayer Lock - Quiet Time is free, iPhone-only for now (Android coming soon), and centers on your own prayer time plus a streak. Prayer Lock: Christian Focus is on iOS and Android, requires a subscription, and adds guided mood-based prayers with a matching Bible verse.

Is Prayer Lock free?
Prayer Lock - Quiet Time (the app on this site) is free to download and use. Prayer Lock: Christian Focus is free to download but requires a subscription to unlock its features.
