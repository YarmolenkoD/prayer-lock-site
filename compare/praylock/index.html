Prayer Lock vs PrayLock: which Christian app blocker is right for you?

A PrayLock alternative · By Daniil Yarmolenko · Updated November 2026

PrayLock unlocks your apps after one daily prayer for your loved ones; Prayer Lock blocks apps for a scheduled prayer session with streaks and history. Both block distracting apps on iPhone through Apple Screen Time, but they answer a different question: do you want a prayer pause every time you open an app, or one protected prayer time each day? Here's an honest comparison.

The core difference

PrayLock : Prayer Screen Time by Ruvix Ltd shields your apps behind a daily prayer. Once each day you pray for yourself, your loved ones or others from a private prayer list (notes require a purchase), and your apps unlock. It also offers daily Scripture verses and prayer topics. As of November 2026 it has around 400 App Store ratings with a 4.8 average. Note that two other apps also called “PrayLock” are Muslim prayer-time apps and unrelated to this comparison.

Prayer Lock protects one dedicated prayer time a day. You schedule it, Focus Mode blocks the apps you chose (social media, news, games), and they stay blocked until you finish and tap “I Prayed”. The rest of the day your phone works normally. A daily streak, prayer history and mood tracking show the habit forming over weeks. Blocking runs on-device through Apple's Screen Time API; Prayer Lock never reads the content of your prayers.

When PrayLock is the better choice

If intercession is the heart of your prayer life and you want a running list of people to pray for, with your apps unlocking after you've prayed through it, PrayLock's prayer-list model is a good match.

When Prayer Lock is the better choice

If you want a set time each day that belongs to God, not just a quick unlock whenever you first pick up the phone, Prayer Lock is the better fit. You choose the time, Focus Mode blocks your distracting apps for the session, and you end it yourself with “I Prayed”. History and mood tracking show how the habit is going over weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Is PrayLock the same as Prayer Lock?
No. There are also two unrelated Muslim prayer-time apps named PrayLock. Prayer Lock - Quiet Time is the app described on this site, App Store id 6758918601.

Does PrayLock block apps for a whole prayer time?
PrayLock's model is pray-to-unlock: pray once and the apps open for the day. Prayer Lock keeps the apps blocked for the length of your prayer session, then unlocks them when you finish.

Is Prayer Lock free?
No. Prayer Lock is a paid subscription: $39.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $7.99/month in the US. PrayLock is free to download with an in-app purchase for extra features.
