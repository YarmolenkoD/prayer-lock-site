Prayer Lock vs GraceLock: which Christian app blocker is right for you?

A GraceLock alternative · By Daniil Yarmolenko · Updated November 2026

GraceLock is a free blocker with 100 written prayers that gates each app open; Prayer Lock protects one scheduled prayer session. Both block distracting apps on iPhone through Apple Screen Time, but they answer a different question: do you want a prayer pause every time you open an app, or one protected prayer time each day? Here's an honest comparison.

The core difference

GraceLock is a Christian app blocker by indie developer Torstein Skulbru. You block Instagram, TikTok and other apps, and before one opens you pray one of 100 written prayers organised by 10 moods. It uses native Screen Time integration and is free to use, with the free tier covering up to three blocked apps according to its listing. It is a new app with only a handful of ratings as of November 2026.

Prayer Lock protects one dedicated prayer time a day. You schedule it, Focus Mode blocks the apps you chose (social media, news, games), and they stay blocked until you finish and tap “I Prayed”. The rest of the day your phone works normally. A daily streak, prayer history and mood tracking show the habit forming over weeks. Blocking runs on-device through Apple's Screen Time API; Prayer Lock never reads the content of your prayers.

When GraceLock is the better choice

If you want something free and you like the idea of a short written prayer each time you open a distracting app, GraceLock is a clean, generous take on that model. Great for a Lent or Advent experiment with zero cost.

When Prayer Lock is the better choice

If per-open prompts feel like a toll you'll learn to skim past, and what you really want is a protected block of time for prayer each day, Prayer Lock is designed for that. Schedule it, let Focus Mode lock your apps, and finish with “I Prayed”. You also get streaks, history and mood tracking to see the habit forming.

Frequently asked questions

Is GraceLock free?
According to its App Store listing, yes: GraceLock is free, with the free tier covering up to three blocked apps. Prayer Lock is a paid subscription with a 7-day free trial.

Does Prayer Lock give you prayers to pray?
Prayer Lock gives you a protected time, not a script. You pray however you pray: your own words, Scripture, a Bible or devotional app. GraceLock supplies 100 written prayers.

Can I run GraceLock and Prayer Lock at the same time?
Both use Apple Screen Time to shield apps, so overlapping rules can conflict. Pick one model: per-open prayers (GraceLock) or a protected daily session (Prayer Lock).
