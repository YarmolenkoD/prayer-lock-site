About Prayer Lock

Updated November 2026

Prayer Lock is a small, independent iPhone app with one job: block the apps that pull you away so that your daily prayer time actually happens. This page explains who builds it, how it works, and how it is funded, so you know exactly what you are installing.

Who builds it

Prayer Lock is designed and built by Daniil Yarmolenko, an independent software engineer. There is no studio or marketing team behind the app; the same person writes the code, answers support email and writes the articles on this site. That keeps the app simple and lets it stay focused on one thing instead of growing a feed of its own.

What the app does

  • Schedule a daily prayer time. Pick the time of day that belongs to God; Prayer Lock reminds you gently.
  • Focus Mode blocks your distracting apps. Social media, news, games: whatever you chose stays locked for the session.
  • Tap “I Prayed” to finish. Apps unlock, your streak grows, and the session is saved to your history with how you felt.

Prayer Lock does not supply prayers or sermons. You pray in your own words, with Scripture, or alongside any Bible or devotional app you already use. If you are choosing between apps, the comparison pages explain how Prayer Lock differs from guided-prayer apps and from other Christian app blockers.

How blocking works, and privacy

On iPhone and iPad, blocking uses Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls and ManagedSettings). The list of apps you block is stored by iOS and never leaves your device; Prayer Lock cannot see which apps you chose, and it never reads the content of your prayers. Details are in the privacy policy. An Android version is in development.

How it is funded

Prayer Lock is a paid subscription with no ads and no sale of data: in the US, $39.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $7.99 a month. Subscriptions are the only revenue, which is why the app can stay quiet and free of sponsored content.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas are welcome. Use the contact form on the home page, or request account and data deletion at any time.
